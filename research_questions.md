Research Questions

Project Title

AI Responsible Electronics: Standards, Safety and E-Waste Research

Selected Focus

E-Waste Management Rules in India and the reliability of AI-generated legal and technical information.

Main Research Question

How can AI tools help students research e-waste management rules in India, and how reliable is the information they provide when compared with official government sources?

Specific Research Questions

1. What is e-waste, and what are its common sources?
2. What are the main objectives of India's E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022?
3. What responsibilities do producers, refurbishers and recyclers have under the applicable rules?
4. How can improper e-waste handling affect human health and the environment?
5. How can students and households manage unwanted electronic devices responsibly?
6. Do different AI tools provide consistent information about Indian e-waste rules?
7. Which AI-generated claims can be verified using official government sources?
8. Can AI tools produce incorrect, outdated or unsupported information about e-waste rules?
9. How can a verification table help identify reliable and unreliable AI-generated claims?
10. What are the limitations of using AI for legal and technical research?

Research Methodology

1. Ask at least two AI tools the same or similar research questions.
2. Record the prompts and important answers from each tool.
3. Identify factual claims that require verification.
4. Compare those claims with official sources, including the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change and the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB).
5. Record each claim's verification status and supporting source.
6. Note incorrect, outdated, conflicting or unsupported claims.
7. Prepare a research report and public-awareness materials based on verified information.

Expected Outcome

This research aims to understand Indian e-waste management, evaluate the reliability of AI-generated information, and demonstrate why important legal and technical claims must be checked against authoritative sources.

Responsible AI Statement

AI-generated information will be treated as a starting point for research, not as final proof. Legal rules may change, so the latest applicable official documents must be checked. This project is educational and does not provide legal advice.
