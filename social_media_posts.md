E-Waste Awareness: Five Social Media Posts

Post 1: Think Before You Throw

📱 Your old electronics still matter!

Broken phones, old chargers and unused computers can become electronic waste. Instead of throwing them away without checking, consider repair, reuse or an appropriate recycling option.

🌱 Small choices can help reduce waste and conserve valuable resources.

Think before you throw. Choose responsible e-waste management!

#EWasteAwareness #ReduceReuseRecycle #GoGreen #Sustainability

---

Post 2: Repair, Reuse, Repeat

🔧 A new device isn't always the answer!

Before replacing an electronic device, ask yourself:

✅ Can it be repaired?
✅ Can someone else reuse it?
✅ Is there a responsible recycling option if it cannot be used again?

Extending the useful life of electronics can help reduce unnecessary waste.

Make your next electronic choice a responsible one!

#RepairAndReuse #EWasteManagement #SustainableLiving #EcoFriendly

---

Post 3: Recycle Electronics Responsibly

♻️ E-waste needs the right destination!

Electronic products may contain materials that require careful handling. Unsafe dismantling and processing can pose risks to human health and the environment.

🌍 Do not burn electronic waste or dismantle it using unsafe methods. Check appropriate collection and recycling options through reliable sources.

Safe handling today helps protect tomorrow.

#RecycleResponsibly #ElectronicWaste #EnvironmentalAwareness #GreenFuture

---

Post 4: Know the Rules

🇮🇳 Did you know about India's E-Waste Management Rules?

The E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 came into effect on 1 April 2023. They establish responsibilities for relevant producers and other entities, including Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR).

Want to learn more? Check the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change websites for official information and updates.

🔎 Check facts before sharing them!

#EWasteRules #EPR #CPCB #ResponsibleElectronics

---

Post 5: AI Helps Research — But Verify!

🤖 Can AI explain e-waste rules? Yes, but don't trust every answer blindly!

AI tools such as ChatGPT and Google Gemini can help students summarize information, generate awareness ideas and organize research.

But AI can make mistakes or provide outdated information.

✔ Compare answers from different tools.
✔ Check important claims against official sources.
✔ Record references.
✔ Be honest about what has and has not been verified.

Use AI to assist your research, not replace fact-checking.

#ResponsibleAI #AIForEducation #FactCheck #EWasteAwareness
