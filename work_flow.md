AI Workflow for E-Waste Research

Project Title

AI Responsible Electronics: Standards, Safety and E-Waste Research

Objective

To use AI tools to research e-waste management, prepare awareness materials, and check the reliability of AI-generated information.

Workflow Steps

Step 1: Define the Research Question

How can AI help students research electronics safety standards and e-waste rules, and how reliable is its legal and technical information?

Step 2: Generate Research Content

Use ChatGPT and Google Gemini to gather explanations about e-waste, recycling, environmental safety, and Indian e-waste rules.

Step 3: Verify Important Claims

Compare important factual claims with official sources such as the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC).

Step 4: Record the Results

Document the claims, their sources, and whether the available evidence supports them in the verification table.

Step 5: Create Awareness Materials

Prepare a research report, awareness article, social media posts, presentation outline, and e-waste awareness poster.

Step 6: Review AI Limitations

Check for unsupported claims, outdated information, missing citations, and errors. Do not treat AI output as automatically correct.

Step 7: Save Project Files

Store the project documents and awareness materials in the GitHub repository for review.

Responsible AI Practices

- Verify important information using reliable sources.
- Do not invent facts, statistics, or references.
- Clearly identify uncertain information.
- Protect personal information.
- Review AI-generated content before submission.

Expected Outcome

A documented research workflow demonstrating how AI can support e-waste awareness while requiring human verification.

Limitation

This document describes the planned workflow. It does not claim that an automated software system has been implemented or tested.
