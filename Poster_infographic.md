THINK BEFORE YOU THROW!

E-WASTE AWARENESS CAMPAIGN

Old electronics don't belong in careless disposal.

WHAT IS E-WASTE?

Discarded electrical and electronic equipment, such as:

📱 Mobile phones
💻 Laptops and computers
🔌 Chargers and cables
📺 Televisions and monitors
⌨️ Keyboards and accessories

WHY SHOULD WE CARE?

- Unsafe handling can harm people and the environment.
- Electronic waste contains materials that need appropriate handling.
- Valuable materials can be recovered through suitable recycling.
- Responsible disposal helps reduce unnecessary waste.

FOLLOW THE 5Rs

REDUCE — Avoid unnecessary electronic purchases.

REPAIR — Fix devices when practical and safe.

REUSE — Pass on working devices through suitable channels.

RECYCLE — Use appropriate collection and recycling services.

RESEARCH — Check reliable sources before sharing information.

KNOW THE RULES

India's E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 came into effect on 1 April 2023.

The rules establish responsibilities for relevant producers and other stakeholders, including Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR).

Check official sources for current requirements and amendments.

AI CAN HELP — BUT VERIFY!

✔ Use AI to understand technical terms.
✔ Compare answers from different tools.
✔ Check legal claims against official sources.
✔ Never assume an AI answer is automatically correct.

YOUR ACTION MATTERS!

Repair when practical. Reuse when possible. Recycle responsibly.
