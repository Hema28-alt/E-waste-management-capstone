Problem Statement

Title

E-Waste Management in India

Introduction

Electronic waste, commonly called e-waste, consists of discarded electrical and electronic equipment such as mobile phones, computers, televisions, chargers, and batteries.

The increasing use of electronic devices has increased the amount of e-waste generated in India. Improper disposal and informal recycling can cause environmental pollution and expose people to hazardous substances.

Problem

Many people are unaware of the correct methods for collecting, recycling, and disposing of electronic waste. Some discarded electronic products are mixed with regular household waste instead of being sent to authorised collection and recycling facilities.

Need for the Project

There is a need to improve public awareness about e-waste segregation, collection, authorised recycling, and responsible disposal. Better awareness can help reduce environmental harm and encourage responsible electronic consumption.

Aim

To study e-waste management in India and develop public-awareness material that encourages safe collection, recycling, and disposal of electronic waste.

Objectives

1. To understand the sources and effects of e-waste.
2. To identify common problems in e-waste disposal.
3. To explain the importance of authorised collection and recycling.
4. To create awareness about responsible e-waste management.

Expected Outcome

The project aims to provide clear, accessible information that helps people understand how to manage electronic waste responsibly.
