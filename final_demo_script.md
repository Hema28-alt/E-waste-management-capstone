Project Title

AI Responsible Electronics: Standards, Safety and E-Waste Research

1. Introduction

Good morning. My project is about using Artificial Intelligence to research electronics safety and e-waste management. The main objective is to understand how AI can help students create awareness while checking the reliability of its information.

2. Problem Statement

Electronic waste includes discarded electronic devices such as mobile phones, computers, chargers, and other electronic equipment. Improper disposal can harm the environment. Students need reliable information about safe collection, recycling, and applicable rules.

3. AI Tools Used

ChatGPT and Google Gemini were selected to support research and content preparation. Their outputs need to be reviewed because AI can provide incomplete, outdated, or incorrect information.

4. Verification Process

Important claims are checked against official sources, including the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC). The verification table records the claims and their supporting sources.

5. Project Deliverables

The GitHub repository contains research documents, a verification table, an awareness article, social media post drafts, a prompt library, an AI tool comparison, a presentation outline, and an awareness poster.

6. Responsible AI

AI is used as a support tool, not as the final authority. Important facts must be verified, references must be checked, and uncertain information must be identified honestly.

7. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how AI can help students research e-waste and prepare awareness materials. Human verification is essential for reliable and responsible use of AI.

Thank you.
