How to Verify a Claim

1. Ask an AI tool the research question.
2. Copy its exact claim into the table.
3. Open the official source and locate the relevant information.
4. Record the official document title and the relevant page, rule or section where possible.
5. Mark the claim as Verified, Partly verified, Incorrect, Outdated, or Unsupported.
6. Explain any differences between the AI response and the official source.
