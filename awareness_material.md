E-Waste Awareness Campaign

Campaign Title

Don't Waste the Future — Recycle E-Waste Responsibly!

Why Is E-Waste Management Important?

Electronic devices contain useful materials, but improper disposal can harm the environment. Responsible collection and recycling help recover materials and reduce pollution.

What Counts as E-Waste?

- Mobile phones and tablets
- Computers and laptops
- Chargers and cables
- Keyboards and electronic accessories
- Televisions and monitors
- Other discarded electrical and electronic equipment

Five Steps to Manage E-Waste Responsibly

1. Reduce: Avoid replacing electronic devices unnecessarily.
2. Reuse: Donate or pass on working devices when appropriate.
3. Repair: Repair devices when it is safe and practical.
4. Separate: Keep discarded electronics away from ordinary household waste.
5. Recycle: Use authorised collection centres or registered recyclers.

What Should We Avoid?

- Do not burn electronic waste.
- Do not dump electronics into water bodies or open spaces.
- Do not dismantle hazardous electronic components at home.
- Do not hand over e-waste to unverified collectors.

Our Awareness Message

Think Before You Throw!

Every electronic device deserves responsible disposal. Make the right choice today to help protect our environment tomorrow.

Suggested Awareness Activities

- Create posters about responsible e-waste disposal.
- Share awareness messages with students and families.
- Conduct a survey about people's e-waste disposal habits.
- Identify authorised e-waste collection options in the local area.

Expected Impact

The campaign aims to improve public understanding of e-waste, encourage responsible disposal, and promote environmentally sound recycling practices.
